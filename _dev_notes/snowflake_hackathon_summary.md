# Snowflake AI & DATA Hackathon 2026 Korea Analysis 

## Overview
Participants will utilize Snowflake's platform and AI capabilities to build AI/data-driven applications and solutions that create tangible business impact. The competition offers two paths: **Tech Track** and **Business Track**.

## 📅 Key Dates
- **April 12 (4월 12일)**: Final Submission Deadline. Teams can iteratively submit to fix bugs, but only the last submission before the deadline counts.
- **April 29 (4월 29일)**: Final Round & Pitch (Seoul). Top teams MUST present their solutions in person; failure to attend disqualifies the team.

## 🎯 Problem Statement (주제 및 해결과제)
Participants can freely select a topic or use provided suggestions. The primary focus is demonstrating clear **Business Impact**.
*Suggested Topics Include:*
- Predicting demand for moving-related services (interior, furniture, water purifiers) and target marketing.
- Analyzing the influence of subway stations or population migration on apartment prices.
- Predicting region-specific telecommunications demand.
- Analyzing seasonal moving trends or rental appliance trends.

## 📊 Available Datasets (제공 데이터)
Datasets are accessed via the **Snowflake Marketplace** and are identical for both Tech and Business tracks.
- **NEXTRADE**: Real-time/closing equity market data, specific stock bid/ask volumes, program trading statistics.
- **RICHGO (Dataknows)**: Seoul apartment prices, real estate index, regional population relocation factors (14+ years of data), and subway proximity data.
- **AJD (아정네트웍스)**: Telecom contracts/subscriptions, rental equipment trends, moving/relocation estimates, and customer service statistics (2022-present).

## 📝 Submission Requirements (필요 제출 사항)
*Final instructions and forms will be emailed specifically to registrants.*
### 1. Tech Track
- **Submission Form & Presentation Template**.
- **Download Link for Source Code (Zip file)**.
- **Demo Video (under 10 minutes)**: Must demonstrate the code, predictive models, and application usage, as well as explain business insights and future strategies.
### 2. Business Track
- **Submission Form & Presentation Template**.
- **Presentation Video (under 10 minutes)**: Highlighting the solution, insights, and marketing/product strategies.
*(Both Tracks require finalists to prepare a 10-minute presentation + 3-minute Q&A for the in-person final round).*

## 🏆 Judging Criteria & Tips (심사 항목)
- **Business Impact**: This is the core theme. The solution must provide tangible value or insights.
- **Snowflake Usage Bonuses**: Utilizing Snowflake features (such as *Snowpark, Snowflake Intelligence, Marketplace, and Streamlit*) grants bonus points.
- **Allowed Languages**: Source code can be written in Python, Java, or Scala.
- **Ecosystem Extensibility**: Permitted to use open-source libraries (APIs, SDKs) and outside partner solutions (AWS, H2O.ai, Dataiku, Datarobot).
- **Execution & Storytelling**: A logical problem-solving approach combined with unique, original storytelling is key.
- **Account Usage Note**: Participants must create a 30-day Snowflake free trial. **Important:** Ensure you backup your code before the trial expires, as expired data won't link to a newly created account.
